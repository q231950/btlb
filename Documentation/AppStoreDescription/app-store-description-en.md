Welcome to BTLB | bɪtlɪb | – your modern study companion for the Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin and the libraries of the Gemeinsamer Bibliotheksverbund!

Term paper, presentation or thesis: BTLB keeps all your sources within reach. Search in seconds, manage your loans, keep track of due dates – simple, clear and right there when you need it.

EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
The Accounts tab shows all the loans and charges of your library accounts in one place. For every item you'll see the title, location, shelfmark, due date and the number of renewals and reservations, so you always know what goes back where and when.

KEEP AN EYE ON LATE FEES
The widget puts your next due date right on your Home Screen. Get reminders whenever it suits you and renew your loans straight from the notification. Or just say "Renew with |bɪtlɪb|" to Siri – done. And of course, renewing in the app takes just a single tap.

RESEARCH MADE EASY
Search the catalogue in seconds and bookmark loans and search results, so your reading list is ready for your next study session. Your search history syncs across all your devices – from your iPhone in the library to your iPad at your desk.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
BTLB is designed to be clean and simple, so you can focus on what matters. Accessibility is close to our heart: Dynamic Type, Light and Dark Mode and iOS accessibility features are supported, so everyone can feel at home in the app.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No tracking, no usage data. Your login details stay safely in your keychain – and nowhere else.

The Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin supports both account sync and search. For the other libraries in the GBV, search is available right now – account sync isn't implemented for every library.

So glad to have you here! Good luck with your studies and happy reading with BTLB | bɪtlɪb |!
